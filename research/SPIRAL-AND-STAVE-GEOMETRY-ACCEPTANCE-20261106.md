# Two independent P2 geometry promotions — acceptance standard

Date: 2026-11-06. Spiral Staircase and Tapered Stave become 30th and 31st active geometry tools ONLY after full browser, mathematical, PDF and offline release checks. All 152 original keyword records stay untouched; keep the Cloudflare pages.dev preview noindex,nofollow,noarchive.

**Spiral Staircase** supports exactly one partial/full turn of 60–360°, a nonzero annular radial tread opening, and risers that include the TOP FINISHED FLOOR. Fifteen risers produce fourteen physical wedge treads, not fifteen wedges. Reference: 108 in total rise / 15 = 7.2 in riser, outer diameter 72 in, pole 4 in, walk-line offset 12 in FROM THE POLE OUTSIDE FACE -> walk radius 14 in. With a 360° total turn across 14 physical treads, each sector = 360/14°, and the TRUE WALK-LINE ARC going = 2π in. The straight chord is shorter. Four top-view local XY corner coordinates and tread elevation per wedge are typed CSV fields; SVG chords are not full curved nosing cutting templates. **Headroom, code clearance, opening, railings, center post engineering and actual tread fixing are outside scope.**

**Tapered Stave** models a straight-faceted regular-polygon FRUSTUM (not a curved-bulged coopered barrel). With top/bottom polygon CORNER diameters 20/16 in, vertical height 24 in and 12 staves, top chord =20sin15°, bottom chord =16sin15°. Face-center slant = hypot(24,2cos15°), CORNER EDGE length=hypot(24,2). These differ and close as edge²=faceSlant²+((topChord−bottomChord)/2)². Plan half-miter=15°; tapered face-to-face theoretical half-dihedral is a separately derived 3D plane angle. These theoretical angles are NOT approved table-saw tilt settings; no finished inner diameter, actual stock nesting or pressure/structural verification. CSV exports one faceted trapezoid per polygon member, with typed length, angle, area and vertex fields. SVG preserves the face aspect ratio, not physical print scale.
