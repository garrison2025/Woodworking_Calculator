# Spiral Staircase and Tapered Stave geometry acceptance

Date 2026-11-06. Two formerly held specialist model types have strictly scoped computational geometry and source-driven canvas/CVS. Original keyword research CSV remains unchanged.

SPIRAL: N explicitly selected annular angular sectors AND equally spaced rise stations span a SINGLE selected revolution of at most 360 degrees. Default rise96in, inner radius3in, outer radius30in, user walkline radius15in, 12 sectors: 30 degrees per sector, 8in modeled vertical station rise, arc at walking line=15*pi/6=7.8539816in, straight walking chord=30*sin(15 degrees)=7.764571in. Every outer/inner start/end polygon point derives from the source polar coordinates relative to center (30,30). The final 12th end elevation is exactly 96in, and the vertices complete one planar turn. Turning direction can be mirrored without changing lengths. This convention is NOT equivalent to every builder's tread/riser/landing count, code walkline, headroom or occupied space.

STAVE: regular STRAIGHT-sided tapered faceted vessel, outside CORNER diameters 24in top and 18in bottom, vertical 30in, 12 flat faces. Let phi=pi/12, ΔR=(24−18)/2=3in. Top/bottom outside chord = 24 sin(phi) and 18 sin(phi). Developed flat face slant = hypot(30,3cos(phi)); corner side edge = hypot(30,3). The invariant hypot(faceSlant,(topChord−bottomChord)/2)=corner side edge independently validates that a planar trapezoid matches the three-dimensional side edge. It is WRONG to use the physical corner edge length as the perpendicular developed face slant. The plan half-wedge angle and half-angle between 3D neighboring surface normals are NOT certified bevel saw settings. Thickness is a reference only.

Source models generate the full numeric rows; the same vertex coordinates produce SVG and length-typed CSV exports. Tests include independent numerical cases, finite bounds, mirrored rotations, stage closure, straight/zero taper, alternative stave counts, rough blank margins, metric round trips and real mobile browser CSV/SVG/validation checks. Printed paper calibration, material trials, code compliance and expert construction signoff are NOT performed.

Technical background: https://nrtechstudio.com/tools/tapered-stave-calculator/ ; https://pavemath.com/spiral-stair-calculator/ . These sources illustrate differing counting conventions; we state ours explicitly rather than claiming safety equivalence.
